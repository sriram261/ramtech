Common questions

Things people ask before signing.

Honest answers to what you're probably already wondering. If something's not here, just ask.

What if my project is smaller than $15K?
We do shorter engagements: single-feature builds, audits, automation scripts, starting around $3K. The $15K floor is for end-to-end products with infrastructure. Tell us the scope and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth a paid engagement or whether you'd be better off with an off-the-shelf tool.
Do you sign NDAs before a discovery call?
Yes. We'll sign yours, or send our standard mutual NDA within an hour. Healthcare, finance, and government-adjacent work always starts under NDA. No additional cost, no review games.
Can you work alongside our existing engineering team?
Often, yes. We own a discrete deliverable (a dashboard, a service, an integration) while your team owns everything else. We work in your repo, follow your conventions, and join your standups when useful. We don't try to take over.
What happens after the project ships?
You get 30 days of priority support included. Bug fixes, monitoring tweaks, onboarding questions. After that, you can either retain us monthly for ongoing iteration, or we hand off cleanly with runbooks, recorded walkthroughs, and a documented architecture. Your call. Most clients keep us on a light retainer.
What kinds of projects do you turn down?
Anything where the timeline is unrealistic for the scope, anything that requires us to ship without auth or logging, and anything that's better solved by an existing SaaS tool. We'd rather lose the work than ship something we wouldn't put our name on.
Are you available for federal or sole-source contracts?
Yes. RamTech Solutions LLC is a US-based, E-Verified Oklahoma company, sole-source friendly, and set up for state and federal procurement workflows. Reach out and we'll send the relevant documentation.
